Feedback from NBCT readers

Get a thoughtful second look at your component drafts before submission.

How to request a reader

If you’re in a cohort

Email your cohort leader to request a reader assignment. Your leader coordinates the match.

About cohort support

If you’re outside a cohort or pursuing MOC

Email the reader coordinator with your name, certificate area, and the component(s) you’re working on.

Email the reader coordinator

What readers provide

  • Up to two reads per component.
  • Feedback through questions that support reflection on your practice.
  • Attention to component requirements and the clarity of your writing.

Readers focus on your response to the prompts and evidence of practice. They do not provide spelling and grammar editing.

Plan ahead

Request a match well before the submission deadline. The program asks candidates to provide a draft at least two weeks before the deadline to allow time for feedback.

All reader assignments are coordinated through a cohort leader or the National Board Leadership Team.
