Boston National Board Program

Your path to National Board Certification

A meaningful next step in your professional growth, supported by a community of Boston educators.

Build on the teaching you do every day.

National Board Certification is a voluntary process through which teachers demonstrate accomplished practice against national standards. It brings together reflection, classroom evidence, and a focus on student learning.

You don’t have to have every answer before you begin. Start by learning about the process, exploring your certificate area, and talking with the Boston team.

Connect your learning to your classroom

The process draws on what you know, how you respond to students, and how you reflect on your instruction. Boston’s support options include component courses, cohorts, trained readers, and writing retreats.

Benefits and professional growth

Certification can support professional recognition, leadership, and ongoing learning. For current information about salary differentials, fee reimbursement, licensure, and graduate credits, use the benefits resources and confirm requirements with the appropriate office.
