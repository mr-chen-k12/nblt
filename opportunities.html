Candidate support faculty

On-call readers

Support candidates by reading draft components and offering reflective feedback. Readers participate in training, follow the program’s feedback protocol and ethical support guidelines, and keep an activity log.

Cohort facilitators

Guide groups of candidates through portfolio instructions and the certification process, build accountability, and connect educators with useful resources.

Component course leaders

Facilitate collaborative professional learning around classroom evidence, teaching practice, and component development.
