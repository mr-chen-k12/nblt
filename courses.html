Learn through your own teaching

Use student work, instructional materials, and classroom video to examine your practice with peers. These courses connect professional learning with work toward a National Board component.

What to expect

  • Facilitated learning with National Board Certified Teachers.
  • Peer feedback on classroom evidence and written reflection.
  • Independent time to select artifacts and develop commentary.
  • A focus on instructional practice and student learning.
